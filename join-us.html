How You Can Help

WAVE volunteers power our events, outreach, and fundraising—supporting pediatric cancer research and uncompensated care at Seattle Children’s.

  • Help run charity events and community booths
  • Coordinate sponsorships and local partnerships
  • Create content to raise awareness
  • Support families with resources and logistics

Student Ambassador Program

WAVE connects high schools across Washington through student-led leadership. Each school appoints three ambassadors who complete monthly outreach, education, or engagement projects—expanding awareness of uncompensated care and inspiring peers to act. Ambassadors gain real-world experience in leadership, communication, and community service while serving as a vital bridge between their school communities and WAVE.

Official Member Program

Our Official Members are the backbone of WAVE. Students take on monthly projects in outreach, fundraising, and community engagement—planning events, staffing booths, and leading key areas. A point-based system tracks involvement and opens leadership opportunities. Members fuel our impact while developing practical skills in leadership, organization, teamwork, and project execution.

WAVE Seattle Administrative Board Application: 2025–2026

Step 1: Submit Your Application

Your application is the first step to joining WAVE Seattle’s Administrative Board. This form helps us understand your interests, skills, and what drives you to make an impact.

  • Deadline: October 5
  • Apply: QR code on our Instagram post, or visit waveseattle.org → Join Us tab.
  • On the site, you’ll find a section-by-section overview of the form.

Tips:

  • Be thoughtful and genuine; share what motivates you.
  • Highlight leadership or volunteer experience.
  • Avoid last-minute submission.
Step 2: Interview with the Executive Board

After you submit, schedule a 20-minute conversation with 3–5 executive board members. It’s friendly and conversational—we want to understand your goals and how they align with WAVE’s mission.

  • Interviews: Oct 6–8
  • Acceptance notifications: Oct 10

Tips:

  • Be yourself—authenticity matters most.
  • Bring examples of teamwork, leadership, or projects.
  • Prepare a couple questions about WAVE.
Step 3: Board Roles Overview
Head of Development
  • Partner with the President to manage the ambassador program.
  • Assign and track monthly projects for ambassadors and members.
  • Build meaningful community connections for WAVE.
Head of Volunteer Connections
  • Recruit, train, and support volunteers.
  • Plan and track members’ monthly projects.
  • Coordinate volunteer efforts at events and initiatives.
Head of Community Outreach
  • Develop and maintain partner relationships.
  • Promote WAVE events, campaigns, and initiatives.
  • Strengthen engagement and visibility for WAVE-led programs.
Final Notes:
  • Review all instructions carefully before submitting.
  • Have questions? Reach out—we’re happy to help.
  • This process helps us get to know you and how you can make an impact with WAVE.

ABOUT WAVE

Founded by Sanjana Medikurthi, WAVE Seattle was conceptualized and developed throughout 2022, before officially onboarding its executive team and members in 2023. Since then, the team has raised over $32,200 for uncompensated care and cancer research at one of the nation’s top pediatric hospitals, growing both its membership and fundraising efforts year over year.
